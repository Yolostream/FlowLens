# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, focus feedback on the work and respect privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing another person's private information and intentionally disrupting community discussions.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact method in the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.
